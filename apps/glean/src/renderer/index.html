Glean picks up every photo and video the page loads, at full size, grouped by post, with captions and dates.

  • Instagram: open the profile, press Auto-scroll. Carousels, reels and stories come through whole.

  • Facebook: a page's Photos tab or posts.
  • Anywhere: Collect, then scroll, or right-click any image.
