Settings

Everything runs on this computer. Bigger models are smarter but slower and need more memory. Downloaded models stay saved, so switching back is instant.

Saved memories

Web access

Web is off until you switch it on in a chat. Searches go straight from this computer to the search engine; the AI itself never leaves your computer.
